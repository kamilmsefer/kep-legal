Privacy policy

KEP Bookkeeper · Last updated October 5, 2026

Who this applies to

KEP Bookkeeper is a private, single-operator tool. It is used only with QuickBooks Online companies owned by its operator, and only with the operator's own authorization. There are no other users.

What data it accesses

With the operator's authorization (QuickBooks Online accounting scope only), it reads vendors, accounts, tax codes and bills, and creates bills, vendor credits and their attachments. It does not access payment information and cannot make payments.

How data is used

Only to record the operator's supplier invoices in the operator's own books. Data is not sold, rented, shared with or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Where data is stored

On a server controlled by the operator. QuickBooks authorization tokens are stored on that server with access restricted to the operator, and are never shared. To do its work, the tool uses the operator's AI model provider (Anthropic) to read invoices and the QuickBooks records it looks up (such as vendor names, account names and bill amounts); that processing is solely to perform the operator's bookkeeping, under the provider's API data terms. No other third party receives QuickBooks data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the operator needs it for bookkeeping. The operator can revoke access at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps), which disconnects the tool immediately.
