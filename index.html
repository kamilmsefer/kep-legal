KEP Bookkeeper

A private bookkeeping tool. Not a product; not offered to the public.

KEP Bookkeeper is a private tool that its operator uses to keep the books of companies the operator owns. It connects to QuickBooks Online with the operator's own authorization, reads the operator's vendors, accounts and bills, and records supplier bills and vendor credits for the operator to review.

It also reads the operator's own Gmail mailbox to sort email and find invoices, and saves summaries and reply drafts there for the operator to review. It never sends or deletes email.

It never makes payments, never deletes or edits existing records, and is not available to anyone else.

Questions: open an issue in github.com/kamilmsefer/kep-legal.
